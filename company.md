# ![](./images/menu/menu_company.png ':size=35') COMPANY

The **Company** section in the `Jimber SASE Platform` centralizes all organizational-level settings and tools. It includes everything from user and group management to domain registration, third-party integrations, notifications, and company-wide configuration. Use this section to structure your network, define access policies, and align the platform with your operational needs.

---

- ![](./images/menu/menu_customers.png ':size=20') [Customers](./company/customers/customers)  
  Manage and oversee your organization's customers, including customer details and associated configurations.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_users.png ':size=20') [Users](./company/users/users)  
  Create, edit, and manage user accounts and roles to control access and security within your platform.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_groups.png ':size=20') [Groups](./company/groups/groups)  
  Define and manage groups to apply consistent network rules and permissions across users and devices.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_domains.png ':size=20') [Domains](./company/domains/domains)  
  Register and manage company domains, which are required to create and authenticate users within your organization.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_integrations.png ':size=20') [Integrations](./company/integrations/integrations)  
  Configure external system integrations such as DNS forwarding, Active Directory, and Microsoft Entra ID synchronization.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_notifications.png ':size=20') [Notifications](./company/notificat/notifications.md)  
  Set notification preferences for your company and individual users to stay informed about system events and statuses.

- ![](./images/menu/menu_configuration.png ':size=20') [Configuration](./company/configur/configuration.md)  
  Manage essential company settings and network isolation configurations to control device access and network segmentation.
